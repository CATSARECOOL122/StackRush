Stack Rush: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

The short version

Stack Rush does not have accounts, does not show ads, and does not track you. Your progress stays on your own device.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (scores, coins, unlocked themes and items, boosts, achievements, settings) in your browser's local storage on your device. We do not receive this data. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, it is deleted. The "Save code" feature in Settings only creates text on your device; sharing it is your choice.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not send your gameplay data, personal information, device identifiers, or analytics to us or to any third party.

Hosting

The game is served as a static website. Like any website host, the hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs (such as your IP address and the time of your visit) for security and operation. We do not have access to a profile of you from those logs.

Purchases

Some optional items can be bought for real money: cosmetic themes and looks, packs of in-game coins, and boosts that help in a round. Payments, if offered, are handled entirely by the payment provider's own checkout (Square on the web, or Google Play Billing in a Google Play version). We never see or store your card details. The game remembers what you bought on your device, and Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases under your Google account. Buying something does not create an account or send us any other data.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, chat, or social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
