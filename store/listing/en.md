## Full description (1729/4000)
Tap to drop the sliding block onto your tower. Land it dead-center for a PERFECT and keep your full width. Miss the edge and the overhang is sliced off. How high can you build?

HOW IT PLAYS
• One tap is all you need.
• Perfect drops build combos, and long streaks make your blocks grow back.
• Land 8 perfects in a row to earn a shield that saves you from a miss.
• The higher you climb, the faster it gets. Stay calm and keep your timing.

7 WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: one life, climb as high as you can.
• Timed Rush: 60 seconds on the clock, perfect drops add time.
• Lives: three lives, only a full miss costs one.
• Hazard: dodge the sliding bombs for bonus points.
• Zen: no speed-up and no game over. Just stack and relax.
• Daily Twist: a new rule every day.
• Campaign: 24 stages in 4 worlds with 3-star goals.

BEAT YOUR BEST
• A ghost of your best run shows the height to beat.
• 26 achievements, levels and a daily reward streak.
• Share a score card with friends in one tap.

15 THEMES
Sunset, Neon, Ocean, Forest, Desert, Ice, Candy, Lava, Sakura, Retro, Deep Sea, Winter Night, plus the premium Gold Rush, Galaxy and Cyber City. Unlock themes by playing, with coins you earn, or buy them. In the shop you can also change block styles, colors, scenery, sounds and music. Optional boosts (Shield, Slow-Mo and Regrow) help in a tough round and are only used up when they actually help; a round with a boost keeps its own best score. Purchases are always optional, with no random paid items.

FAIR AND FRIENDLY
• No ads.
• Works offline.
• No account needed. Your progress stays on your device.
• A short practice round for new players and an optional timing assist.
• 13 languages, plus reduce-motion and sound options.
